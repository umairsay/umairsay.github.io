FIFA World Cup ’26 — Player Archetype Atlas

Built from FIFA’s post-match report release for the 2026 World Cup, across all 104 matches. Every measure is a percentile among players at the same FIFA position, from the 606 outfield players with at least 120 tournament minutes. Goalkeepers are a separate group of 48, at 180 minutes or more. Similar players come from cosine similarity on the role measures.
Positions are preserved exactly as reported in FIFA’s official match reports. Archetypes are derived from observed tournament function.
